## Introduction
Deploying a website can feel intimidating, but with the right tools, you can get your site live for free in minutes.

## Pick the Right Free Hosting Platform
- **GitHub Pages** – Ideal for static sites built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.  
- **Netlify** – Offers continuous deployment, form handling, and a global CDN.  
- **Vercel** – Great for Next.js projects and serverless functions.  
- **Firebase Hosting** – Perfect for single‑page apps and real‑time databases.

## Step‑by‑Step Deployment Guide
1. **Create a Repository** – Push your site files to GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket.  
2. **Connect the Repo** – In Netlify/Vercel, link your repo and set the build command (`npm run build` or `jekyll build`).  
3. **Configure Domain** – Use the default `*.netlify.app` or `*.vercel.app` subdomain, or point your custom domain via DNS.  
4. **Deploy** – Click “Deploy” and wait for the build to finish.  
5. **Verify** – Open the URL, check console logs, and ensure assets load correctly.

## Optimize for Performance
- Minify CSS and JavaScript.  
- Enable image compression (WebP, AVIF).  
- Leverage caching headers and a CDN.

## Wrap‑Up
Free hosting platforms give you a powerful launchpad without cost. Experiment with different services, keep your code in version control, and enjoy a live website that scales with your traffic.
